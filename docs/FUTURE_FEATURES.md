# EXMOB - FUTURE FEATURES

Good ideas, deliberately postponed. Nothing here is required for the Cabin. Add to this list
rather than implementing early.

## Gameplay
- Melee finisher when an enemy is within 1 m and the magazine is empty.
- Throwable: improvised (bottle) to break a window on purpose / distract.
- Guard dog with a zone and a single stun.
- Hired muscle AI defender with a simple follow/hold command.
- Weapon upgrades (damage, mag, reload, spread).
- Ammo types (buckshot vs slug).
- Perks tied to story moments (small, permanent).
- Wave mutators (power cut, storm, no shop, night rain).
- Hearing model for AI (gunfire updates last-known position).
- Captain buffs and role assignment per group.
- Driver archetype with mid-wave reinforcement vehicles and drive-bys.
- Arsonist area denial (fire propagation on wood - big feature, own design doc).
- Sniper sightlines and laser warning.
- Breacher with faster door destruction and shutters counter.
- Enemy retreat and regroup when a group loses its leader.

## Property
- Barricade furniture (drag the couch to a door).
- Shutters, cameras with a HUD minimap, alarm, exterior lights that can be shot out.
- Safe room and gun/medical cabinets usable during combat.
- Fire damage and scorch marks.
- Structural damage stages for walls.

## Presentation
- Skeletal animation from Blender rigs (replacing procedural part animation).
- Ragdolls on HIGH/ULTRA.
- Bloom and light-shaft post-processing on desktop tiers.
- Rain and snow weather.
- Radio/TV audio inside the cabin.
- Reactive dialogue barks with a small VO budget.
- Photo mode.

## Platform
- Cloud saves.
- Gamepad support.
- PWA install / offline.
- Localization.
- Achievements and statistics screen.
- Accessibility: aim assist on touch, color-blind palettes, remappable keys.

## Deliberately rejected (for now)
- Online multiplayer.
- Base-building / crafting.
- Open world.
- Vehicles driven by the player.
