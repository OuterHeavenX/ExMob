# EXMOB - ART DIRECTION

## Style

**Stylized cinematic realism.** Believable proportions, slightly exaggerated readable
silhouettes, high-quality PBR materials, strong clothing design, excellent lighting, and
characters that read at elevated camera distance. Target quality: premium modern indie / AA.

Not photorealism. Not cartoon. Think of a crime film graded for night.

## The first screenshot rule

The first Cabin screenshot must say "premium crime-action survival game", not "Three.js demo".
Composition, lighting, fog, proportions, materials, and dressing carry that before any asset is
final.

## Lighting

Lighting is the number one visual priority.

Cabin palette:
- **Interior**: warm. Tungsten lamps (2700K-ish, orange-amber), a warm kitchen light, a bedside
  lamp. The interior is the "safe" color.
- **Exterior**: cold. Blue-gray moonlight, deep blue-black shadows, fog catching light.
- **Porch light**: a single warm bulb that spills onto the steps - the threshold between safe and
  dangerous.
- **Headlights**: harsh, white-yellow, raking through the trees, casting long shadows.
- **Muzzle flashes**: brief warm-white bursts that light the darkness for a frame or two.

The contrast between warm safe interior and cold dangerous exterior is EXMOB's visual identity.
It carries through every chapter (the mansion is warmer and the enemies colder).

## Color

- Ray: charcoal coat, oatmeal sweater, dark trousers. Muted, reads as a solid dark silhouette
  against warm floors.
- Goons: cheap loud accents (burgundy, teal, mustard) on black. Readable and a little ridiculous.
- Enforcers: black on black, wide.
- Soldiers: slate gray tactical civilian wear.
- Hitman: black coat, black hat, pale face - the one silhouette that is thin.
- Environment: wood browns, wool, worn fabric, rusted metal, snow-blue exterior.

## Materials

PBR (metal/rough). Wood grain on walls and floor, worn fabric on the couch, dull metal on the
fridge, cheap plastic on the phone. Roughness variation is what sells cheap versus expensive
across chapters. Texture budgets in PERFORMANCE_BUDGET.md.

## Characters

Silhouette first. Every archetype must be identifiable at 13 m elevated camera distance in
motion, at night, in fog. Then color. Then detail.

Ray: broad shoulders, long coat, slight forward lean, one hand near a pocket. Older. Heavy.

## Environments

Composition from the game camera. The Cabin is framed so the driveway leads the eye from the
bottom of the screen to the porch light, and the forest closes in on the sides. Fog compresses the
distance so the treeline reads as a wall.

Dressing: enough props to make a life legible (coffee cup, clipping, suitcase, photograph,
map, mail) without cluttering movement.

## VFX language

Short, bright, then gone. Muzzle flashes are the brightest thing on screen for two frames.
Impacts are surface-specific. Smoke lingers and catches light. Blood is dark and matte, never
glossy, never excessive.

## UI

Minimal, cinematic, typographic. Condensed uppercase for banners. Warm off-white text on near-
black. Red is reserved for damage and the bounty.
