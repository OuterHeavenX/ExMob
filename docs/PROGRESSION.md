# EXMOB - PROGRESSION

## Character progression

Ray does not level up. He gets better equipment, better armor, a better home, and more help.
His personal stats (health 100, speed) stay constant so the game stays readable. Optional future:
small permanent perks tied to story moments (documented in FUTURE_FEATURES.md).

## Weapons

Cabin: Pistol (start) -> Revolver -> Shotgun -> SMG. Unlocks are purchases. Later chapters add
rifles, better pistols, and per-weapon upgrades (damage, magazine, reload, spread) with registry
costs.

## Armor

Body armor is a purchasable layer above health. It absorbs a percentage of incoming damage until
depleted and is replaced, not repaired. Later chapters add heavier vests.

## Properties

Cabin -> Small House -> Townhouse -> Luxury Home -> Estate -> Mansion. Each property is
purchased with cash and comes with its own defense slots and upgrade tree.

## Security / defenses

Unlocked by chapter (see PROPERTY_SYSTEM.md). Defenses purchased on one property do not transfer;
knowledge of them does (the shop remembers what has been unlocked).

## Campaign progression

Chapter -> wave index -> phase. Saved after every cleared wave. Chapter end triggers the
COMPROMISED sequence and the relocation beat.

## Statistics (tracked in save, shown later)

kills by archetype, shots fired, accuracy, waves survived, cash earned, cash spent, doors broken,
windows boarded, deaths, time played.
